    How it works

    From a client's own wearable to a coordinator's decision, in six steps.

    Prototype with simulated data

    01

    Client

    Consumer wearable

    Apple Watch, Garmin or Fitbit, the device the client already owns. Everwell is hardware-agnostic.

    02

    Vendor

    Companion app / vendor cloud

    The watch syncs to its own app and cloud as usual: steps, sleep, resting HR, night wakings, HRV.

    03

    Integration

    Aggregation API (Terra)

    One API normalises data from every brand into the same daily metrics.

    04

    Platform

    Everwell, EU hosting

    Data is stored and processed only in the EU.

    05

    Analysis

    Detection engine

    • 28-day personal baseline
    • z-score
    • EWMA
    • change-point

    Each client is compared with their own usual range, not a population norm. Sudden changes, slow drifts and missing data are flagged.

    06

    People

    Care coordinator

    Sees a ranked list with plain-language reasons, then decides whether to visit, call or wait.

    The human always decides

    Everwell ranks care visits; it does not diagnose. Every action is chosen and recorded by a coordinator.

    Privacy by design

    • Health data under GDPR Art. 9
    • Explicit consent from each client
    • EU data residency
